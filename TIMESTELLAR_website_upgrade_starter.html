In 2011, two friends lit a telescope room with a single desk lamp.

One was an astronomer. The other repaired watches.

They wanted a watch that stays readable under open sky.

So they learned to charge lume until it outlasted the night.

Every TIMESTELLAR is still assembled by one watchmaker, from first screw to final signature.

We make fewer than we could sell. Time is not something to rush.
